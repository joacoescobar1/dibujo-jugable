## Pitch
A kid draws a map with colored pencils: the river, the dog, grandma's house. Snap a photo and seconds later they are playing it. A kid draws on paper; Meta models turn the drawing into a playable level, then an agent *plays* it, sees it fail, patches it, and replays until it's winnable. Unlike Kitchen Game (prompt → game, no proof it's beatable), **we play the level before shipping it**. If the drawing has a river cutting across the whole map or a fence around the goal, the agent detects it by playing, proposes a minimal fix (a bridge, an opening in a wall) that respects the drawing, and plays again. You see it on screen: broken attempt → fix → WIN. Every level we ship comes with a verified win trace.

## North-star metric
**% of drawings that end up playable and winnable with no human edits** (after-loop win rate), alongside first-try win rate.
Current (offline, mock, no Meta; `evidence/eval.json` 2026-10-28): first-try 0.48 (11/23) → after-loop 0.96 (22/23); the new item synthetic_12 starts broken and ends winnable (the only loss: `survive-trap`, impossible by design).

## Running the demo
- `xdg-open game/demo.html` (`file://` works, screenshots embedded), or
- `scripts/serve_demo.sh 8000 [--rebuild]` → http://127.0.0.1:8000/demo.html (`?iter=0` = broken attempt, `?iter=0&tour=1` or the **Judge mode** button = automatic fail→fix→win tour)
  - On the board: the agent hits the bug (🐞), you see what the fix changed (🔧 arrow/bridge/door), and the WIN keeps the drawing visible (ribbon, not a curtain). Guard: `tests/demo/test_demo_legibility.py`.
- Rebuild: `.venv/bin/python scripts/build_judge_demo.py` (default `samples/synthetic_12`: synthetic kid-style drawing — rocket, rocks, purple monster on the star — generated by `scripts/make_kid_sample.py`; `--sample samples/synthetic_11` = same story in geometric shapes; `--sample samples/synthetic_08` = fence) + `scripts/shoot_judge_demo.py` → evidence/judge_demo.png
- Full offline pipeline: `scripts/demo.sh`

## Meta models
- **Muse Spark (Meta Model API, Responses)**: drawing vision → GameSpec JSON + loop patches.
- **SAM**: segment strokes (river, walls, characters).
- **Voice/transcribe**: the kid narrates the rules ("if the dog touches you, you lose").
- **Image edit**: sprites from the original strokes.
- **Muse Code**: harness development.

## Spend policy
Smoke test first, capped loops per run, usage logged locally. Sealed verification oracle (`evidence/oracle.sha256`) so the model cannot "win" by editing the checker.
